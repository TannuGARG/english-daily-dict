# Daily English — 2026-10-19

Focus: daily personal use + working professional English.

## Words (10)

### 1. prioritize
- **Meaning:** To decide what is most important and do that first.
- **Usage:** I need to prioritize the client email before starting new work.

### 2. clarify
- **Meaning:** To make something clearer or easier to understand.
- **Usage:** Could you clarify the deadline for this task?

### 3. follow-up
- **Meaning:** An action or message that continues an earlier conversation.
- **Usage:** I'll send a follow-up after today's call with the next steps.

### 4. reliable
- **Meaning:** Someone or something you can trust to work well.
- **Usage:** She's very reliable — if she says she'll finish it, she will.

### 5. flexible
- **Meaning:** Able to change easily according to the situation.
- **Usage:** My schedule is flexible this week if you want to meet earlier.

### 6. considerate
- **Meaning:** Careful not to inconvenience or hurt others.
- **Usage:** It was considerate of him to message before dropping by.

### 7. concise
- **Meaning:** Short and clear, without unnecessary words.
- **Usage:** Please keep the update concise — three bullet points are enough.

### 8. proactive
- **Meaning:** Taking action early instead of waiting for problems.
- **Usage:** Being proactive about blockers saves the whole team time.

### 9. appreciate
- **Meaning:** To recognize the value of something or thank someone for it.
- **Usage:** I really appreciate your help with the presentation.

### 10. postpone
- **Meaning:** To delay something until a later time.
- **Usage:** Can we postpone dinner to 8 PM? I have a late meeting.

## Phrases (10)

### 1. on the same page
- **Meaning:** Sharing the same understanding or agreement.
- **Usage:** Let's quickly align so we're on the same page before the review.

### 2. touch base
- **Meaning:** To check in briefly with someone.
- **Usage:** Can we touch base tomorrow morning about the release?

### 3. keep me in the loop
- **Meaning:** Keep me informed about updates.
- **Usage:** Please keep me in the loop if the plan changes.

### 4. at your earliest convenience
- **Meaning:** As soon as you reasonably can (polite).
- **Usage:** Please review the draft at your earliest convenience.

### 5. circle back
- **Meaning:** Return to a topic later.
- **Usage:** Let's circle back on the budget after we get the estimate.

### 6. make time for
- **Meaning:** Arrange your schedule so you can do something.
- **Usage:** I want to make time for a walk after work today.

### 7. dig into
- **Meaning:** Look at something carefully in detail.
- **Usage:** I'll dig into the error logs and share findings by EOD.

### 8. take ownership
- **Meaning:** Accept responsibility for delivering something.
- **Usage:** She took ownership of the onboarding checklist and finished it.

### 9. catch up later
- **Meaning:** Talk or meet another time to update each other.
- **Usage:** I can't talk now — can we catch up later this evening?

### 10. out of office
- **Meaning:** Not available at work (away / leave).
- **Usage:** I'm out of office on Friday, so please ping Ankit for approvals.
