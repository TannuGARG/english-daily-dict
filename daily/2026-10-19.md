# Daily English — 2026-10-19

## Words (10)

### 1. meticulous
- **Meaning:** Extremely careful and precise about details.
- **Usage:** She was meticulous about checking every line of the report before sending it.

### 2. concise
- **Meaning:** Giving a lot of information clearly in few words.
- **Usage:** Please keep your summary concise — one paragraph is enough.

### 3. resilient
- **Meaning:** Able to recover quickly from difficulties.
- **Usage:** The team stayed resilient after the production outage and fixed the issue overnight.

### 4. ambiguous
- **Meaning:** Open to more than one interpretation; unclear.
- **Usage:** The requirement was ambiguous, so we asked the product manager to clarify it.

### 5. pragmatic
- **Meaning:** Dealing with problems in a practical way rather than theoretically.
- **Usage:** He took a pragmatic approach and shipped a simple fix first.

### 6. articulate
- **Meaning:** Able to express thoughts clearly and effectively.
- **Usage:** She articulated the customer pain points very clearly in the meeting.

### 7. inevitable
- **Meaning:** Certain to happen; unavoidable.
- **Usage:** With that much traffic growth, a capacity upgrade was inevitable.

### 8. feasible
- **Meaning:** Possible and practical to do.
- **Usage:** Migrating the whole system in one weekend is not feasible.

### 9. discrepancy
- **Meaning:** A difference between two things that should match.
- **Usage:** We found a discrepancy between the invoice total and the payment amount.

### 10. escalate
- **Meaning:** To raise an issue to a higher level of authority or urgency.
- **Usage:** If the vendor does not reply by EOD, we should escalate to their manager.

## Phrases (10)

### 1. on the same page
- **Meaning:** In agreement; sharing the same understanding.
- **Usage:** Let's sync for five minutes so we're on the same page before the client call.

### 2. touch base
- **Meaning:** To make brief contact to update or check in.
- **Usage:** Can we touch base tomorrow morning about the release checklist?

### 3. in the long run
- **Meaning:** Over a long period of time; eventually.
- **Usage:** Refactoring now will save us time in the long run.

### 4. at a glance
- **Meaning:** With a quick look; immediately obvious.
- **Usage:** The dashboard shows system health at a glance.

### 5. cut corners
- **Meaning:** To do something poorly or incompletely to save time or money.
- **Usage:** We shouldn't cut corners on security just to meet the deadline.

### 6. keep someone in the loop
- **Meaning:** To keep someone informed about progress or decisions.
- **Usage:** Please keep me in the loop if the scope changes.

### 7. hit the ground running
- **Meaning:** To start a job or project with energy and competence from day one.
- **Usage:** With good onboarding docs, new joiners can hit the ground running.

### 8. take something with a grain of salt
- **Meaning:** To not fully believe something; to be skeptical.
- **Usage:** Take early performance numbers with a grain of salt — the sample size is small.

### 9. get the ball rolling
- **Meaning:** To start a process or activity.
- **Usage:** I'll send a draft agenda to get the ball rolling.

### 10. under the weather
- **Meaning:** Feeling slightly ill.
- **Usage:** I'm a bit under the weather today, so I may join the call muted.
