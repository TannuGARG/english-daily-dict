# Daily English — 2026-10-27

Focus: intermediate vocabulary for daily personal life + working professionals.

## Words (10)

### 1. assert
- **Meaning:** To state something confidently and firmly.
- **Usage:** In the review, she asserted that the delay was caused by missing requirements.

### 2. comply
- **Meaning:** To follow a rule, request, or instruction.
- **Usage:** All vendors must comply with the new data-privacy guidelines.

### 3. deplete
- **Meaning:** To use up or reduce something until little remains.
- **Usage:** Back-to-back meetings can deplete your energy by afternoon.

### 4. endeavor
- **Meaning:** An attempt to achieve something difficult; to try hard.
- **Usage:** Moving cities for work is a big endeavor, but it can open new opportunities.

### 5. fulfill
- **Meaning:** To complete a duty, promise, or requirement successfully.
- **Usage:** We need two more documents to fulfill the KYC requirements.

### 6. grasp
- **Meaning:** To understand something fully; to take hold of something.
- **Usage:** It took a while to grasp how the new billing flow works.

### 7. immerse
- **Meaning:** To involve yourself completely in an activity or environment.
- **Usage:** Immersing yourself in English podcasts for 20 minutes a day really helps.

### 8. mandate
- **Meaning:** An official order; to require something by rule.
- **Usage:** The company mandated two-factor authentication for all employees.

### 9. nurture
- **Meaning:** To help something grow or develop carefully over time.
- **Usage:** Good managers nurture talent instead of only pointing out mistakes.

### 10. pinpoint
- **Meaning:** To find or describe the exact cause or location of something.
- **Usage:** Logs helped us pinpoint why the payment callback was failing.

## Phrases (10)

### 1. a blessing in disguise
- **Meaning:** Something that seems bad at first but later turns out helpful.
- **Usage:** Missing that flight was a blessing in disguise — we avoided the storm.

### 2. burn the midnight oil
- **Meaning:** Work late into the night.
- **Usage:** The team burned the midnight oil to finish the release notes.

### 3. catch someone off guard
- **Meaning:** Surprise someone when they are not prepared.
- **Usage:** The sudden client question caught me off guard in the demo.

### 4. go against the grain
- **Meaning:** Do something that feels unnatural or against usual practice.
- **Usage:** Skipping the weekly status call goes against the grain, but we needed deep-work time.

### 5. have second thoughts
- **Meaning:** Start doubting a decision you already made.
- **Usage:** I’m having second thoughts about booking the expensive hotel.

### 6. in the nick of time
- **Meaning:** Just before it is too late.
- **Usage:** We submitted the form in the nick of time before the portal closed.

### 7. jump to conclusions
- **Meaning:** Decide something too quickly without enough evidence.
- **Usage:** Don’t jump to conclusions — the drop in traffic may just be a tracking bug.

### 8. keep your cool
- **Meaning:** Stay calm under stress.
- **Usage:** Keep your cool during the escalation call and stick to the facts.

### 9. once in a blue moon
- **Meaning:** Very rarely.
- **Usage:** We eat out once in a blue moon; weekdays are usually home food.

### 10. stick to your guns
- **Meaning:** Continue supporting your decision despite pressure.
- **Usage:** Stick to your guns if you believe the timeline is unrealistic.
