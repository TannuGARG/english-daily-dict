# Daily English — 2026-10-20

Focus: daily personal use + working professional English.

## Words (10)

### 1. confirm
- **Meaning:** To make sure something is true or agreed.
- **Usage:** Can you confirm if the meeting is still at 3 PM?

### 2. available
- **Meaning:** Free to talk, meet, or help.
- **Usage:** I'm available after 5 PM if you want to discuss this.

### 3. prefer
- **Meaning:** To like one option more than another.
- **Usage:** I prefer morning meetings so afternoons stay free for deep work.

### 4. update
- **Meaning:** The latest information about something.
- **Usage:** Just sharing a quick update — the document is ready for review.

### 5. responsible
- **Meaning:** In charge of a task or accountable for it.
- **Usage:** I'm responsible for sending the weekly status report.

### 6. convenient
- **Meaning:** Easy and suitable for your schedule or situation.
- **Usage:** Is 7 PM convenient for a quick call, or should we pick another time?

### 7. suggest
- **Meaning:** To offer an idea or recommendation.
- **Usage:** I'd suggest we finish the draft today and review it tomorrow morning.

### 8. urgent
- **Meaning:** Needing attention or action very soon.
- **Usage:** This isn't urgent — you can reply when you're free.

### 9. improve
- **Meaning:** To make something better.
- **Usage:** A short practice session each day can improve your spoken English.

### 10. arrange
- **Meaning:** To organize or plan something.
- **Usage:** I'll arrange a cab so we reach the office on time.

## Phrases (10)

### 1. as soon as possible
- **Meaning:** At the earliest time you can (often written ASAP).
- **Usage:** Please share the revised file as soon as possible.

### 2. let me know
- **Meaning:** Tell me / inform me.
- **Usage:** Let me know if you need any help with the presentation.

### 3. looking forward to
- **Meaning:** Feeling happy or eager about something coming up.
- **Usage:** Looking forward to catching up this weekend.

### 4. in progress
- **Meaning:** Currently being worked on; not finished yet.
- **Usage:** The fix is in progress — I'll update you once it's done.

### 5. no worries
- **Meaning:** It's okay; don't feel bad about it.
- **Usage:** No worries if you're late — we can start without you.

### 6. by the way
- **Meaning:** Used to add a related or side comment.
- **Usage:** By the way, did you book the restaurant for Friday?

### 7. take a look
- **Meaning:** Check or review something.
- **Usage:** Can you take a look at this email before I send it?

### 8. works for me
- **Meaning:** That plan or time is fine for me.
- **Usage:** 10 AM works for me — see you then.

### 9. reach out
- **Meaning:** Contact someone for help or information.
- **Usage:** Feel free to reach out if you have any questions.

### 10. wrap up
- **Meaning:** Finish or bring something to an end.
- **Usage:** Let's wrap up this discussion and continue tomorrow.
