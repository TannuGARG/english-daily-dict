# Daily English — 2026-10-21

Focus: intermediate vocabulary for daily personal life + working professionals.

## Words (10)

### 1. align
- **Meaning:** To bring people, goals, or plans into agreement.
- **Usage:** Let’s align on the release date before we tell the client.

### 2. delegate
- **Meaning:** To give a task to someone else so work is shared.
- **Usage:** I’ll delegate the slide design to Riya and focus on the numbers.

### 3. escalate
- **Meaning:** To raise an issue to a higher level when it needs more attention.
- **Usage:** If we don’t get a reply by evening, we should escalate to their manager.

### 4. constrain
- **Meaning:** To limit what is possible because of rules, time, or resources.
- **Usage:** The budget will constrain how many features we can ship this quarter.

### 5. infer
- **Meaning:** To reach a conclusion from clues, not from direct statements.
- **Usage:** From her tone, I inferred she wasn’t happy with the delay.

### 6. accommodate
- **Meaning:** To adjust plans so you can help someone or fit a need.
- **Usage:** We can accommodate a 7 PM dinner if that works better for your parents.

### 7. substantiate
- **Meaning:** To support a claim with facts or evidence.
- **Usage:** Can you substantiate this estimate with last month’s metrics?

### 8. tentative
- **Meaning:** Not final; likely to change.
- **Usage:** Friday is still tentative — I’ll confirm once the tickets are booked.

### 9. overwhelm
- **Meaning:** To make someone feel that something is too much to handle.
- **Usage:** Too many parallel tasks can overwhelm even a strong team.

### 10. reciprocate
- **Meaning:** To respond to a kind action with a similar one.
- **Usage:** They hosted us last time, so we should reciprocate and invite them over.

## Phrases (10)

### 1. ballpark figure
- **Meaning:** A rough estimate, not an exact number.
- **Usage:** I don’t need the final cost yet — just a ballpark figure is fine.

### 2. move the needle
- **Meaning:** To make a noticeable improvement or impact.
- **Usage:** Small UI tweaks won’t move the needle; we need a clearer value proposition.

### 3. call it a day
- **Meaning:** Stop working for now; finish for the day.
- **Usage:** We’ve fixed the critical bugs — let’s call it a day and continue tomorrow.

### 4. bear with me
- **Meaning:** Please be patient while I do something.
- **Usage:** Bear with me for a minute while I pull up the latest report.

### 5. in the meantime
- **Meaning:** During the period before something else happens.
- **Usage:** The approval is pending; in the meantime, I’ll prepare the rollout checklist.

### 6. raise a red flag
- **Meaning:** Signal that something looks wrong or risky.
- **Usage:** The sudden drop in conversions should raise a red flag for the growth team.

### 7. speak up
- **Meaning:** Express your opinion clearly, especially when something matters.
- **Usage:** If the timeline feels unrealistic, please speak up in the planning meeting.

### 8. cut to the chase
- **Meaning:** Skip the extra talk and get to the main point.
- **Usage:** I’ll cut to the chase — we need two more engineers for this launch.

### 9. on the same wavelength
- **Meaning:** Thinking in a similar way; understanding each other easily.
- **Usage:** Once we talked it through, we were finally on the same wavelength.

### 10. leave room for
- **Meaning:** Keep some flexibility or space for something.
- **Usage:** Don’t pack the agenda fully — leave room for unexpected questions.
